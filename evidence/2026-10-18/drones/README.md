# 3D models of the drones, v0.9 lane D

One picture per drone, taken from the real drone picker of a local release by `tools/bench/src/accept-v09-drones3d.ts` (evidence `../v09-drones3d.json`). The picture is the whole stage: the turntable at its first view, the numbered pins and the legend beside it. Colours, finishes and the layout of wires and screws are stylised; sizes follow the published dimensions (docs/research/drone-models.md).
