## 0. Root cause of "N cycles only 3-4 scenes" (item 9)

Measured on the live catalogue (`GET /api/catalog`, 2026-10-17): **13 published scenes, 4 with
`collision: true`** (887f27aa, 39e63ce9, 7a475d38, b11e45d1) and **9 with `collision: false`** (no walls
file published; the browser bakes walls on demand, D32).

`SceneRotation.candidates()` (`packages/scenes/src/rotation.ts`) dropped every scene whose walls flag was
`false` unless the setting `scenes.allowNoWalls` was on, and its default is **off**. N (`scenes.order`
default `random`) drew from a shuffle bag of the 4 that remained, so a visitor saw 3-4 scenes. Since v0.7
(item 4) the picker shows every published scene whatever its walls (`TAB_FILTERS.showcase` has no walls
control), but the rotation was never brought in line: the two disagreed, and the owner's published scenes
were invisible to N, to Shift+N and to the scene after a crash. Not the cause (checked): `/api/catalog`
answers all 13 (the picker shows them), `hidden` scenes are left out by the API, the 24 h failed-load
skip touched none of them, the recent list is not read by the curated source, `flown`/`kind` filters are
the picker's own and never reach the rotation. `showcase.json` (the release's static fallback) carries only
3 scenes: N would see just those if `/api/catalog` did not answer in 4 s (`loadCatalog`), but the owner's
picker lists all 13, so the API answered for him.

Fix: the owner's catalogue is never filtered by walls in the rotation (the picker's rule, `tabObeys`);
the walls rule stays for the pilot's own sources (history, favourites). Regression test with the live
13-scene flags and a negative control (the v0.8 rule gives 4).

## 1. One ordered source: the tour (`packages/scenes/src/tour.ts`, pure)

N and "the scene after a crash" (`scenes.autoSwitch`) walk the **tour**:

1. **The catalogue**, in what the visitor sees on the picker's catalogue tab: pinned (Featured) scenes
   first, then the rest, each in the admin's order (`catalogueOrder`, tested equal to `storefront()`);
   `hidden` out, scenes that failed to load in the last 24 h out; **walls never filter it**.
   `scenes.order` sequential (the new default): the first unseen scene after the current one, round the
   list; random: a uniform draw among the unseen.
2. **SuperSplat**, once every catalogue scene was seen: pages of the SuperSplat tab's defaults
   (`defaultFilters` + `toQuery`: trending, all time, walkable unless the walls filter or
   `scenes.allowNoWalls` lets wall-less in), read lazily through `/api/superspl/explore` (one page of
   24 at a time, shared with the tab's session cache), in the list's order. Skipped: our own catalogue
   ids, scenes already seen, scenes failed lately, formats the simulator is not known to open
   (`formatVerdict !== 'opens'`, the same rule as the tab's random pick).
3. **Nothing left** (every scene seen, or SuperSplat did not answer): a new round from the catalogue;
   the tour says so (`again`) and the page flashes why.

**Seen** = every scene on screen (the one the page opened with, N, a pick, a link). **No repeats until all
are seen**: the seen set and the SuperSplat stream are only ever extended inside a round.

**Between visits nothing is remembered** (decision D45, changed after the review): every page load walks the
owner's list from its start. The first version kept the place for 30 days (`gsfpv.tour.v1`) and credited that to
the owner; his message 22 (items 9 and 11) does not ask for it, and it works against item 9 (a second-day N from the
first scene would land in SuperSplat and look like the old bug). The capability is still in the pure tour: with a
`store` it keeps `{ v, at, seen: [catalogue ids], ss }` (`ss` = entries of the SuperSplat list consumed, resumed at
page `floor(ss/24)*24`; a record older than 30 days is ignored; a catalogue scene added since is unseen). The page
does not hand one in (`PageTourOptions.remember`, off); turning it on is that one flag. Owner question: does he want it.

**The wait for SuperSplat is bounded** (decision D47, added after the review): the 12th press is the only one that
goes to the network. All the pages of one step share a 4 s budget (`SUPERSPLAT_READ_MS`); on no answer the step falls
back to the catalogue (`again`, the page says so), a page that lands late is dropped and read again by the next step
(nothing in the list is skipped). While it waits `SceneHost.busy` is true (N ignored), so a read slower than 0.6 s
flashes "Looking for the next scene..." (`rotation.looking`, 4 languages).

Defaults: `scenes.order` default `random` -> `sequential` ("his curated list, his order" in the plan; the verbatim
says "random or just next, I do not know", so this is the lane's choice and the owner can flip it), no schema bump
(an unset default is not stored). Because of that, `orderFor(source, chosen)` (`rotation.ts`) gives an UNSET order
"in order" only to the owner's catalogue; favourites and history keep the shuffle they had (a history walked in order
ping-pongs between the two scenes flown last: opening a scene moves it to the top; reviewer finding, reproduced and
tested). A stored choice (also one equal to the default) or a URL override applies to every source. The help text of
`scenes.order` says so. Option text for `scenes.rotation` `curated` becomes "The author's list, then SuperSplat".
`favourites` and `history` keep the old rotation code (walls rule and all). Shift+N random keeps the old shuffle bag,
now over the whole catalogue (the same root cause hit it).

## 2. Item 11: the hint (`app/builtin/next-hint.ts`, pure rule in `app/next-hint-logic.ts`)

- Counted: crashes of the visit (not replays), across scene switches. **3rd crash**, and **6th if N was
  still not used**: a small non-blocking line "Want another place? Press N for the next scene".
- **Never again once N was used** (`gsfpv.nextUsed.v1`, guarded localStorage): set by every N (key, radio
  switch, crash-panel button, the hint's button) and Shift+N, in `SceneHost.go`. Not shown when
  `scenes.autoSwitch` is on (the crash already goes to the next scene).
- Touch: the text without a key plus a **Next scene** button. Radio with a mapped `scene.next` switch
  (`hasFn`): "flip your next-scene switch (or press N)". Otherwise the key line.
- Placement (each spot measured in a browser, checks H1, H4, H5): desktop and tablet: bottom centre one step above
  the crash toast. A narrow phone (<= 640 px wide, with the pads or without): at the top, under the toast's room
  (the toast is at the top without pads; with pads the toast AND the recording bar sit above them, ~130 px of
  buttons of varying height, and a spot above the toast covered REC, Auto and the folder button: H4). A phone on
  its side with pads (<= 500 px tall): one line right under the toast, in the strip between the pads (above the
  pads it fell off the top of the screen: H5). `pointer-events: none` except its own buttons. The crash panel
  (Replay, Rewind, Start, ... in the middle) would be covered by any spot that fits every screen, so the hint
  steps aside (`display: none`) while the panel is open: the panel has its own Next scene (N) button. A first
  version at the top centre did cover the panel (H1). The bounding-box check proves the hint intersects no
  visible button, pad (as a circle), text block, the crash toast or the panel.
- Fades after 12 s, on a scene switch, on N, on its close button. `role="status"`.
- 4 languages: `packages/i18n/locales/fly/rotation/*.json` (new keys `nexthint.*`).
