## Findings

### D2 (fixed): a line of the lesson that was left ends the NEW lesson's speech early (`sonic/bridge.ts`)

- Defect: `SonicBridge.start()` resets the speech count to 0 for every lesson (`SonicMode.enter()`), but a `say()` of the lesson that was left is still in flight (G, or a lesson chosen on the start screen over the flight) and when it ends (cut by speechSynthesis.cancel, or on its own) its `finally` took one off the new lesson's count and, at 0, sent a `speech.end` marker and called `runner.spoken(t)` of the NEW runner while the new lesson's own line was still being said: the runner goes on before its intro is over, and the recording carries a marker that is not the host's real end of speech.
- Test that failed before the fix (and passes after): `tools/bench/test/sonic-bridge.test.ts` "review D2" (unit, fake engine: a `speech.end` at t 0 appeared while the new lesson's line was in the queue) and `tools/bench/test/sonic-lessons.test.ts` "review D2" (the real host, mode, bridge and runner on the real flight model; only the engine and the drawing are fakes): G while the intro of rings-first is being said, then the glossary's first three `speech.end` markers: `[true, true, true]` for "the engine is still speaking" before the fix, `[false, false, false]` after. Controls that pass both before and after: the same lessons with no lesson left behind (clean open of ears-glossary), lines of one lesson counted as before, a line ending after `stop()` says nothing.
- Fix: a run counter (`epoch`, bumped by `start()` and `stop()`); a `say()` takes its own run's number and only a line of the current run changes the count or reports the end of speech.
- Checks run: `sonic-bridge` (15), `sonic-lessons` (23), `sonic-mode` (5): 43 pass; typecheck of `@gsfpv/fly` and `@gsfpv/bench`.

### D2b (fixed): G, or a lesson chosen on the start screen, does not cut what the lesson left was saying (`sonic/lessons.ts`)

- Found while proving D2: after the D2 fix the first `speech.end` of the glossary opened with G at t 16 ms came at 75 s of page time, a clean open's at 12.9 s (probe on the lesson rig, FakeEngine queued like speechSynthesis). A lesson says 4 to 6 lines at once when it opens (probe over the 20 analytic lessons: 4 lines in 16 of them, 5 in four, 6 in the corridor), the runner marks none of them `interrupt` at `begin()` (only N and an answer interrupt, `interruptNext`), so the new lesson's lines queue BEHIND the old intro: a blind pilot who presses G to reach the glossary, or picks another lesson, first hears the rest of the old lesson's intro (here about a minute).
- Test that failed before the fix: `sonic-lessons.test.ts` "review D2b" (the first line of the glossary opened by G while the intro of rings-first is being said has `interrupt: true`, the other lines do not, and its first end of speech comes within 20 s). Control that passes before and after: a lesson opened while nothing is being said (a clean open, or after the previous one finished) queues its lines with no interrupt.
- Fix: `LessonHost.open()` reads the bridge's speech queue before `mode.enter()`; when it is not empty the first `say` of the new runner carries `interrupt` (one flag, cleared after the first line and after `enter()`).
- Checks run: `sonic-lessons` (25), `sonic-bridge` (15), `sonic-mode` (5): 45 pass; typechecks.

### D4 (fixed): over a running flight the start screen cannot be operated with Space or the arrow keys (`devices/keyboard.ts`, one selector)

- Defect: the lane drove the start screen by keyboard only at the entry, before the flight exists. Over a flight (the pause menu's "Fly by Sound: lessons and settings", the end of the ladder, a lesson that did not open) the keyboard source is live and, in the game scheme (`input.play` easy, which the mode forces), reads Space, the arrows and W A S D Q E as flying keys and calls `preventDefault()` on them unless `dialogOpen()` says a dialog is up. `dialogOpen()` knew `.screen.radio, .panel, .screen.drones, .screen.scenes` and not the start screen. Effect: Space on "calm mode", "captions", "my screen reader speaks" or "motor sound" does nothing, the arrow keys do not move the path radio group or a slider, Space does not press a button or open the layers control (only Enter works), and every one of those presses is also flying input to a flight that is paused behind the screen. The same function gates P (pause menu), B (feedback), L (share), O (settings) and the mouse-look pointer lock, so they also reached past the screen.
- Probe (refuting attempt, real page: headless Chrome 156, Vite dev server, keyboard only, `tools/bench/src/sonic-review-keys.ts`): before the fix 5 of 14 checks pass: every Space or arrow keydown on the screen is `defaultPrevented`, calm mode stays off, the path stays full, the speaking-speed slider stays at 1, the headphone button is not pressed, the details control stays closed. After the fix 14 of 14 (calm toggles on and off, the radio group goes to fast, the slider 1 to 1.2, the headphone status says "Playing a sound on your left.", the details control opens, Escape closes the screen and the flight goes on, 0 mouse events, 0 page errors). Control kept: with the screen closed Space is still the flight's key and still prevented (the keyboard flies as before).
- Test that failed before the fix: `tools/bench/test/sonic-review.test.ts` "review D4" (dialogOpen() with a shown `.sonic-start` is true, with a hidden one false; controls: the four old dialogs still count, a loading screen and the captions do not). Evidence: `evidence/2026-11-07/sonic-review-keys.json` (the 14 of 14 run).
- Fix: `.sonic-start:not([hidden])` in `dialogOpen()`. This is the one change outside the lane's own files: `devices/keyboard.ts` is shared and the plan names no owner for it; one selector, merge risk small. The alternative inside the lane's files (the screen swallowing the keys with a capture handler that runs before the keyboard source's) depends on listener order and leaves P, B, L and O open.
- Checks run: keyboard-related and sonic tests (9 files, 159 pass), typechecks.

### D1 (fixed): Fly by Sound remembers its keyboard stand-in over the pilot's radio (`app/flight.ts`, `app/input.ts`)

- Defect: with no radio found, `fly()` wrapped the result of `resumeInput()` so that a keyboard came out in its place (`asKeyboard`), and `InputHost.start` then did what it does for a found input: `use(c)` with remember on, `saveLastInput({ kind: 'keyboard' })`. The plain simulator's own fallback, `openRadio`'s onClose, flies the keyboard as a stand-in with `use(c, false)` exactly so that the radio of last time is looked for again. After one Fly by Sound visit with the radio not found (not plugged in yet, asleep, a helper starting the page first) `radioProfiles.last` was `{ kind: 'keyboard' }`, `resumeInput()` answers a keyboard at once for that, the radio is never looked for again, in Fly by Sound or in the plain simulator, until the pilot opens Controls and picks it by hand: the owner's radio is the done-means of the stage.
- Probe (refuting attempt, real page: headless Chrome 156, Vite dev server, a prefs document whose last input is a TX16S, no radio connected): with the pre-fix `flight.ts` and `input.ts` put back, the page's stored `radioProfiles.last` after entering a lesson is `{"kind":"keyboard"}` (14 of 15 checks of `sonic-review-keys.ts`, the failing one this); with the fix it is still the TX16S (15 of 15).
- Test that failed before the fix: `tools/bench/test/sonic-review-input.test.ts` (4 tests become 5 with controls): `InputHost.start(resumed, standIn)` with nothing found flies the keyboard with its note, opens no Controls screen and leaves `last` as the radio. Controls that pass before and after: a radio that is found flies and is remembered; the plain simulator with nothing found opens the Controls screen and keeps the radio; the mechanism (a keyboard handed to `start()` as the input found, the old call, is remembered over the radio); the keyboard picked on the Controls screen is the pilot's choice and is remembered.
- Fix: `fly()` passes the keyboard as `standIn` (the second argument of `InputHost.start`, used only when nothing was found and the Controls screen is not shown) and `use(standIn, false)`; the wrapper `asKeyboard` and its import are gone. `controlsNext` and every other line of `fly()` are as before; with the mode off `standIn` is null and `start()` is as it was.
- Checks run: `sonic-review-input` (5), `app-known-controller`, `app-switches`, `app-modes` (45 in four files) pass; typechecks; the page probe above.

### D7 (fixed): R held while the window loses focus becomes the long press (`sonic/mode.ts`, `app/builtin/sonic-flight.ts`)

- Defect: R's short and long press is decided by `pressAt` (set on keydown) and cleared by the keyup the feature listens for. A window that loses focus with R down (an alt-tab, a system dialog, a hidden tab) never sends that keyup; the frame clock goes on, so `pollR` turns the press into the long press (back to the lesson's start) 0.7 s later, or at once when the tab becomes visible again. The keyboard source forgets its held keys on blur for the same reason; the mode did not.
- Probe (real page: headless Chrome 156, Vite dev server; R down, a `blur` event on the window, 1.5 s, then the keyup, `tools/bench/src/sonic-review-keys.ts`): with the pre-fix feature file the mode's `reset.start` key event appeared while the window was blurred (events before 0, during 1, after the keyup 1); with the fix 0, 0, 0 (17 of 17 checks). Control kept: a quick R is still one reset to the checkpoint.
- Test: `tools/bench/test/sonic-mode.test.ts` "review D7" (the real session and mode: R down, `cancelR()`, three long-press lengths of flight, no key event, the late `releaseR()` adds none; control: the same hold without it is `reset.start`). Before the fix `cancelR` did not exist, so the unit test fails on that; the behaviour before the fix is the page probe's.
- Fix: `SonicMode.cancelR()` drops the held R; the feature calls it on window blur and when the tab is hidden.

### D5 (fixed): the mode's keys: a held N runs through a lesson, and Shift+N and Shift+F still switch the scene (`sonic/mode.ts`, locales)

- Defects, two in one table (`SonicMode.enter()`): (a) the N handler (`scene.next`) ignored `e.repeat`; every other key of the feature checks it (R, Z, I, G). In the ears lessons (module 0) `LessonSession.next()` skips the line, scene or question going on, so a held N (auto-repeat) skips the rest of the lesson, questions included (answered as skipped). (b) The mode took N from the scene rotation and nothing else of it: Shift+N (a random scene) and Shift+F (the next favourite) still ran `ctx.scenes.go(...)` from `app/builtin/rotation.ts`. Shift is the game scheme's descent key and the router reads Shift+N as that chord first (`shiftIsFlying` falls back to the key alone only when the chord matches nothing), so a pilot who is descending and presses N, to skip an intro, replaces the lesson's world with a catalogue scan while the lesson's runner goes on in the old one (its `place` actions then move the scan craft to lesson coordinates). The plan's own rule is "N = next step, never a scene switch in the mode". Not touched: the pause menu's scene items, which are the helper's deliberate choice.
- Test that failed before the fix: `tools/bench/test/sonic-mode.test.ts` "review D5" (the real session and mode, the key table as the router runs it): a held N (one press, ten repeats) gave 11 key events before the fix, one after; Shift+N called the rotation's handler before and is the next step after; Shift+F called it before and is the note `sonic.sceneLocked` after; `leave()` gives both handlers back and they run again.
- Fix: N ignores repeats; `scene.random` is the next step (`KeyN`, `next`); `scene.favourite` shows the note; new key `sonic.sceneLocked` in en, es, pl, ru.
- Checks run: `sonic-mode` (6), `sonic-lessons` (25), `packages/i18n` (22) pass; typechecks; gates architecture, catalog and B18 PASS.

### D6 (fixed): in "my screen reader speaks" mode a lesson's title and first lines never reach the screen reader (`sonic/captions.ts`, `sonic.css`)

- Defect: with `selfVoice` off the engine says nothing and the captions region is the pilot's only voice, a polite live region. ## Risks for the next agent

- The `main` chunk is +105 302 bytes gzipped (+13.1 %) for every pilot, with the mode never used. A `?sonic=`-only dynamic import would take the package, the lessons and the start screen out of it, but `sonicFlight` installs synchronously with a teardown, `sound.ts` and `fly()` read `sonicEntry()`, and `features.ts` is the lead's seam: a design change, not a one-line fix; measure first (`node scripts/build-release.mjs`, compare `dist/fly/assets/main-*.js`).
- Each lesson is a new `enter()`: a new bridge clock (`t` from 0), a new `lesson open`. A recorder that keeps one package across the auto-advance of a path would write frames whose time goes back to zero: it must cut a package at each `lesson open` (the replay core says a package is one lesson).
- `ctx.keys.on` runs the LAST handler registered: the recorder lane's `I` must say the lesson line first, and any new scene-changing action registered by another lane needs the same care as `scene.next`, `scene.random` and `scene.favourite` in `SonicMode.enter()` (D5).
- With "my screen reader speaks" the lesson can go on after the longest single line while the reader is still on the intro (the package's parallel waits); a host-side queue in `SonicBridge.say()` would fix it for that mode, but it changes when `speech.end` is sent, so it needs the recorder lane's agreement.
