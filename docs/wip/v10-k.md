# v0.10 lane K: a "How to fly" card at the start of the game

The owner's message 23, item 1 (`docs/wip/v10-plan.md`): "There is NO information that Shift flies down and Space flies up,
for gamers who play on the keyboard. When the game opens it must remind and show this, so people do not search for how to
control the game. With a 'do not show again' checkbox." Base: `198b659` (v0.9 live). Builds on `docs/wip/v09-b.md` (the game
scheme, D57-D62) and `docs/wip/v08-a.md` (the key card).

## 1. What a new visitor sees, in order (measured, not guessed)

`tools/bench/src/probe-v10-k-first-visit.ts` on a local release of the base build (198b659), system Chrome, a fresh browser
context, no `?nowarn`; screenshots and the DOM list of every guidance surface in `evidence/2026-10-20/v10-k/first-visit-base/`
(`v10-k-first-visit-base.json`), the 900 x 480 window in `first-visit-base-short/`.

| # | Desktop (Chrome, WebHID present) | Says Space = up, Shift = down? |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | "Before you fly": the motion notice, one button | no |
| 2 | the loading card | no |
| 3 | Controls "The scan is ready. How will you fly?": four device buttons; the green primary one is **Radio or controller over USB**, **Keyboard is the fourth and plain**; under it one muted 13 px line says "Space up, Shift down" | only that small grey line |
| 4 | after Keyboard (or x): the key card, a translucent box over the lower middle of the scan, **nine rows of equal weight** (Mouse, W A S D, arrows/Q/E, Space, Shift, F, Esc, R, K), the title "Click to fly" | row 4 and 5 of 9, same size as "R back to the start" |
| 5 | take-off (the first flying key): the card goes. The corner line reads `K keys · F full screen · R respawn · Esc pause · H hide text` | **no**: nothing names the vertical pair for the rest of the flight |

Phone (390 x 844, touch emulation without WebHID, as Android Chrome): no Controls screen, the pads and a one-line hint
("Left stick: up / down and turn. Right stick: fly."), "Touch a stick to take off". Fine, and it is the touch version of the card.

### Why the keys are not noticed (root causes)

- **R1. The keys sit behind a choice the gamer may not make.** The keyboard is the last button of a dialog whose accent points at a
  radio. A gamer who clicks the green one, "Touch sticks" or the x lands in a different place; only the muted line under Keyboard
  names Space and Shift. A pilot who flew before never sees the dialog again (the input from last time comes back by itself),
  and a pilot with a radio never sees any key text at all.
- **R2. The card that exists is a status card, not a guide.** Nine rows with the same weight, translucent over a busy scan; the pair
  that is new to a gamer (vertical flight) is not marked. It is only for the keyboard source, and it goes with the first key.
- **R3. Nothing in the air.** After take-off the corner line has no Space and no Shift, and K (the only way back to the card) is
  one item of that line.
- **R4. A real bug on a short window.** `wizard.css`: `@media (max-height: 500px) { .kc-list li:not(.now) { display: none } }`
  hides every row of the game card (it has no "now" rows) and leaves "Pilot keys as on a radio (Mode 2, Space arms)". At
  900 x 480 the card shows the line "Click to fly" and not one key (`first-visit-base-short/`, "Space and Shift both in the page
  text": false). A laptop window with DevTools open is that short.
- Not a cause, checked: the card is not covered by the motion notice or the Controls dialog (they come before it and the card is
  hidden behind Controls on purpose); the card is shown for a keyboard on the ground (`say` is set).

## 2. Design

**One card, "How to fly"** (`apps/fly/src/ui/controls-card.ts` + `.css`, logic in `controls-card-logic.ts`, page half in
`app/builtin/how-to-fly.ts`). A modal dialog on the existing `panel()` (role dialog, aria-modal, x, Esc through the pause key,
`dialogOpen()` true so flying keys and the pause menu stay out of it, the flight paused with the `panel` reason).

- **When.** Once per page visit, when the scan is ready, an input is chosen, and nothing else is up (no Controls screen, no panel,
  no pause menu, no picker, no crash panel, no loading card, tab visible) for 350 ms. So it never stands on the motion notice
  (that one is before loading) or on the Controls dialog, and it follows the choice made there. Not under `?nowarn=1`, `?simradio`,
  `?lat=1` (test pages: the 40 benches that pass `nowarn` keep their flows) and not when `input.howToFly` is off.
- **What, by the input in use** (`cardKind(source, input.play)`): `game` (keyboard, easy play: the hero pair, then Mouse, W A S D,
  turn keys, F, Esc, N, R, K), `pilot` (keyboard, Mode 2: Space arms, W/S, A/D, arrows, M, R, Esc, N, K), `touch` (the two pads
  drawn, left/right, "touch a stick to take off"), `radio` (a radio or gamepad: sticks as set up, arm, mode, N, Esc, K).
- **The pair.** Two big key caps on top of the game card: **SPACE** with an up arrow and "UP", **SHIFT** with a down arrow and
  "DOWN, hold to land"; green and amber, but every signal has a word and an arrow too (not colour alone).
- **One source of truth for key names.** Caps of the flying keys are derived from `GAME_KEYS` / `MODE2_KEYS` (`play/keys.ts`);
  F, Esc, N, R, K from the keymap (`keysFor`, `ctx.keys.caps`). A unit test fails when the card's caps and the tables differ.
- **Start flying** closes the card and, for the game scheme, captures the mouse (the pause release asks `mouse.lock()` inside
  the click; `play.ts` already does it for every resume). **Do not show again** is the pref `input.howToFly` (global bool, on by
  default, "Show the How to fly card when the game opens", also in Settings); the box writes it at once, so x and Esc keep it.
- **K and the pause menu** open it any time (K toggles; the menu item "How to fly" shows K beside it). It replaces the old K
  (pin the ground key card), whose game variant is removed: the card's rows moved here, so there is no second list of the keys.
  The pilot keyboard's ground card stays (it carries the live arm steps).
- **Accessibility.** Focus goes to Start (restored on close), Tab stays inside, Esc and x close, caps and text on the panel's
  dark colours (contrast >= 7:1), the checkbox is a real labelled input, reduced motion: no animation.
- **In the air, game scheme.** The corner line starts with `Space up · Shift down`; for the first 8 s of the first flight of a
  visit a small pill shows the two caps (it goes at once when Space or Shift is pressed; not shown after "do not show again").
- **R4 fixed** by the new card's own CSS; the old `max-height: 500px` rule only touches the pilot ground card.

## 3. Acceptance

`tools/bench/src/accept-v10-keys.ts` (SITE required; the base build is the negative control, `V10_CONTROL=1`): a fresh visitor sees
the card with Space and Shift after the scan is ready and not before the motion notice is dismissed; it never covers the Controls
dialog; Start flies (the mouse is captured, the drone takes off on the first key); with the box ticked the card does not come back
after a reload; K opens it again; the pause menu item opens it; touch emulation shows the touch version; a radio profile shows
the radio version; the 900 x 480 window shows the keys; the first-flight pill shows and goes. Unit tests: logic, caps, policy.
