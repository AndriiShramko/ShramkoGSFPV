# v0.7 lane E — wind of the place and the collision ceiling (design note)

Owner's item 10 (a pilot on LinkedIn): the wind of the place, editable by hand because most scans have
no location ("if the wind always blows from the north, the scan could have it too"), and collisions
off above a height for outdoor scans (he crashed into floating sky splats). Contract fields are on the
base (`packages/scenes/src/catalog.ts`): `wind {fromDeg, speed, gust, source}`, `compassDeg`,
`ceilingM`, `geo`; lane A serves `GET /api/wind/<id>?mode=now`.

## 1. Frames and units (what the contract comments now say exactly)

- **Scene frame** = the flown world: PlayCanvas, +Y up. HUD heading 0 is the scene's forward axis
  `-z`; the heading grows turning right (clockwise seen from above), as `attitude().yaw`. Heading psi
  points along `(sin psi, 0, -cos psi)`.
- **`compassDeg`** = the compass bearing of heading 0. A bearing `B` is the scene heading
  `B - compassDeg`. A scene without it counts its forward axis as north (the UI says so).
- **Wind `fromDeg`** = where the wind comes FROM (weather-report convention); the mean wind blows toward
  `fromDeg + 180`. `speed` = mean at 10 m above the ground, `gust` = amplitude around the mean, m/s.
- **`ceilingM`** = a height in the scan's own coordinates (the world before the E.7 scene scale
  `T(w) = s*w + t`, scale 1), metres along +Y. Flown height = `s * ceilingM + t_y`. A voxel is ignored
  when its lower face is at or above the ceiling; a voxel the plane cuts through still collides.
- **Scene scale (x0.25..x100): wind is the drone's air.** Physics stays in metres and the scene scales
  around the drone (E.7), so wind speed is real m/s around the real-size drone and never scales with
  the scene (a x10 scene does not blow 10x harder; the pilot asked what a <250 g drone does in wind).
  The gradient's height above ground is in flown metres from the scan's ground, which follows `T`.
  Gust time scales are seconds. The ceiling is part of the scan (its floaters are), so it scales with it.

## 2. Wind in sim-core (`packages/sim-core/src/wind.ts`, new)

- `WindConfig {fromDeg, speed, gust, compassDeg, seed, groundM}` is `ParamOverrides.wind`: wind is a
  parameter of the flight model, so it is in every life header (`overrides`, covered by `configHash`)
  and a change is a 'life' change (a new life where the craft is, A.7). No wind in the overrides =
  exactly today's model: every scene without wind, and the A6 hash `1d01ca5b...` stays.
- `WindField.at(tick, heightAboveGround, out)`: mean along the downwind unit vector (compiled once with
  `dsin`/`dcos`), plus gusts from **stateless** seeded value noise of `(seed, axis, tick)` (integer hash,
  Catmull-Rom over knots every 4 s / 1.3 s / 0.4 s): along-wind `gust * n`, cross-wind `0.6 * gust * n`,
  vertical `0.3 * gust * n` (estimates). Stateless means: no new hashed state slot, the same gusts at
  every frame split, after a rewind and from a trimmed life's snapshot. The along-wind total never
  reverses (clamped at 0).
- **Gradient**: power law `(h / 10 m)^(1/8)` (Hellmann exponent 1/8, open terrain; three `Math.sqrt`,
  so no `log`/`pow` in the step), `h` clamped to 0.5..200 m (x0.69 at the ground, x1.45 at 200 m).
  `h = py - (s * groundM + t_y)`: `groundM` is the floor under the spawn in scan coordinates (the
  session probes it once per set of walls; no walls or no floor: the spawn's height), and
  `Sim.setSceneFrame(s, ty)` gets the scene transform from the life header and every world record (the
  runner and the replays set it, so the gradient follows a rescale without a new life).
- **Applied through drag on the airspeed**: the existing quadratic body drag and the rotor/duct
  momentum drag take `v - wind` instead of `v`. With no wind `v - 0 === v`, bit for bit.
- `Sim.windNow` (not hashed) holds the last wind vector for the HUD.

## 3. Log format /3 (`runner.ts`)

- Writers write `gsfpv-input-log/3`; readers take /2 and /3 (old logs replay unchanged: /2 has no wind
  and no ceiling by construction). A /2 header that carries `overrides.wind` or `scene.ceilingM` is
  refused (it cannot be a real /2 log). An older page refuses /3 by format, cleanly, instead of
  replaying it without the wind. `SIM_CORE_VERSION` is not bumped: with defaults no trace changes.
- World record (kind 2): `ch[5]` = ceiling (scan metres, Float32), `ch[6]` = flags, bit 0 = ceiling on
  (a /2 record has 0 there: no ceiling). `WorldEvent.ceilingM: number | null`; the life header's scene
  gains `ceilingM?` (absent = none).

## 4. The ceiling in packages/collision (`src/ceiling.ts`, new)

- `withCeiling(scanWalls, ceilingM)`: the same octree arrays (nothing copied) with the voxel rows at or
  above the ceiling empty, through `isVoxelSolid` (the layer analytic worlds already use, so every
  vendored query, `querySphere`, `queryRay`, `isFreeAt`, and our swept test see it; vendored code
  untouched). The cut is computed in index space on the scan's own walls (format 1.0's flipped y
  handled), so `transformCollision` carries it to any scene scale exactly.
- Order everywhere (session and replay share `session/world.ts`): floater filter -> ceiling -> scene
  transform. `dropFloaters` refuses a ceilinged input (it needs the plain octree). Octree walkers see a
  ceilinged collision as an analytic world (correct, voxel by voxel); the overlay therefore draws the
  scan's walls and tints the rows above the cut (per-quad class 2 next to the floater flag).
- Respawn and rewind are unaffected beyond the walls they query: spawn search, push-out and the
  director ask the same flown collision; walls off still means no contact world at all.

## 5. Settings (`packages/prefs/src/defs/wind.ts` new, `scene.ts` extended), no schema bump

- `wind.mode` global, `off | scene | now | custom`, default `scene` (= off for a scene without wind).
- `wind.fromDeg`, `wind.speed`, `wind.gust` per scene (custom), defaulting to the scene's catalogue
  wind (`curated: 'wind.speed'`: the app's resolver reads nested catalogue fields), else 0 deg, 4, 2.
- `scene.ceiling` per scene, `scene | off | custom`, default `scene`; `scene.ceilingM` per scene, scan
  metres, default the catalogue's `ceilingM`, else 10 000 m (a ceiling that ignores nothing). The
  height is set in a panel with a live preview (voxels above tinted, a translucent plane), shown to the
  pilot as metres above the start at the current scene size.
- Both apply as 'life' settings: while paused they wait, on resume one new life where the craft is.

## 6. App

- `app/builtin/wind.ts`: resolves the source (pure `session/wind-source.ts`, unit-tested): scene ->
  catalogue wind; now -> `GET /api/wind/<id>?mode=now` (timeout; failure falls back to the scene's
  wind, else none, with a note); custom -> the settings. The first life already has it (the session
  awaits it with the walls). HUD dial `ui/wind-dial.ts`: arrow where the wind blows relative to the
  nose + m/s when wind is on, an N on the ring when `compassDeg` is known.
- `app/builtin/ceiling.ts` + `ui/ceiling.ts`: the setting's row extra and the panel; preview through
  `VoxelController.previewCeiling`, the plane through render-pc `CeilingPlane` (new, public engine API
  only). Session: `setCeiling` (world record + new life, like the floater filter), `setWind`.
- Optional "save as this scene's default" for an admin session: decided at the end (see summary).

## 7. Tests (each with a negative control)

- sim-core: wind changes the trajectory with wind on, not with wind off (control: a zero-wind config
  equals no wind bit for bit); same seed same trajectory, another seed another; frame splits agree;
  the noise is a function of (seed, tick) (control: shuffled call order gives the same values); the
  gradient follows a rescale; /3 log with wind replays to the live hash (control: the header's wind
  edited -> configHash refuses); an old /2 log (fixture written by the base commit's code) replays to
  its saved hash; a /2 header with wind is refused; A6 reference hash pinned in a vitest.
- collision: a voxel above the ceiling is ignored, one below collides (sphere, sweep, ray); a voxel the
  plane cuts collides; flipped format; scale x0.25/x4/x100 equals the unscaled answer through `T`
  (control: applying the ceiling after the transform, in flown metres, answers differently).
- app (tools/bench/test): source resolution (now / fallback / custom / off); the session's world for
  a replay rebuilds the same ceilinged walls (control: without the record's ceiling the hash differs).
