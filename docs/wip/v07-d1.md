# v0.7 lane D1 — flight UX: sharing everywhere, recording on every scan, crosshair, motor sound

Plan: `docs/wip/v07-plan.md` (the owner's message 18, items 6 and 2; the Telegram ideas R-20261006-0001..0003:
a crosshair in the centre, the drone's motor sound). Branch `v07-d1`, base `cbc41e8`.

## 1. Sharing is never hidden in a menu (item 6)

Today: one path, `app/builtin/share.ts` — the pause menu's "Share this scene" and the summary panel's
"Nice flight" row (after 30 s). Nothing on the flight view, the crash panel, the stats card, after a
recording, or in the picker.

**One share module, three files** (the old builtin is split, not duplicated):

| File | What | DOM |
|---|---|---|
| `apps/fly/src/share/links.ts` | which link a share carries per entry point, the words per kind, the target URLs, the Instagram caption | none (Node tests) |
| `apps/fly/src/share/picture.ts` | the 1200x630 picture of the pilot's view with the brand and the scan's credit (moved from the builtin) | canvas |
| `apps/fly/src/share/panel.ts` | the one share panel: native share sheet, copy link, LinkedIn, Facebook, X, WhatsApp, Telegram, Reddit, Instagram (save the picture or video + copy the caption), save picture, the video file | yes |
| `apps/fly/src/app/builtin/share.ts` | the flight feature: where the panel opens from | yes |

**Entry points** (`ShareEntry`): `top` (a button always on the flight view's top bar: icon + "Share" on a
desktop, the icon alone on a phone), `key` (L, the keymap's free letter: "link"), `pause` (the pause
menu, which is also the summary panel, and its "Nice flight" row), `card` (the stats card after a
disarm), `crash` (the crash panel), `video` (after a recording stops: "Share your flight"), `picker`
(every card of the picker: catalogue, recent, favourites, Continue, the SuperSplat tab) and `recent`.

**The link** (contract with lane A, `docs/wip/v07-plan.md`):
- In flight (`top key pause card crash video`): the view as it is becomes the picture, `POST /api/share`
  answers `/s/<id>` (unchanged server). When no picture can be made or the API fails: the scene page.
- From the picker (`picker recent`): no flight, no picture: the scene page.
- The scene page is `/{lang}/scenes/<id>/` for a scan of the owner's catalogue (lane A builds those
  pages); a scan outside the catalogue has no page, so its link is the simulator's deep link
  `/{lang}/fly/?scene=<id>` (keeping `budget` / `stochastic` of the page's own URL, lane R's contract).

**Words** make FPV pilots want to try it, per kind: `flight` (a line I flew, can you?), `crash` (the
scan won at N m/s, fly it clean), `scene` (fly this real place in your browser), `video` (my line,
your turn). en/es/pl/ru in `packages/i18n/locales/fly/share/`. Instagram has no web share intent: its
button saves the picture (or the video) and copies the caption (words, link, the scan's credit, tags).

**Video** (after a recording): the saved file goes through `navigator.share({ files })` where
`navigator.canShare` allows it (phones); elsewhere "Save video" + copy link.

## 2. Recording on every scan (item 2; supersedes D34's gate)

`cinema.ts` / `builtin/recording.ts` / `builtin/cinema.ts` / `builtin/video-export.ts` refuse a scan
outside the showcase. The gate goes: `Recording.allowed` is "the browser can encode video".

- **Scene facts** (`apps/fly/src/scene-facts.ts`, pure): title, author, licence from the catalogue, else
  from what the picker's SuperSplat tab knew when the pilot picked it (kept in the scene library's
  `author` / `license` fields, which exist and were never written: `rememberFacts` in
  `packages/scenes/src/library.ts`), else only the id.
- **Credit burned into every video**: `<title> — <author>, <licence> · ShramkoGSFPV · gsfpv.flyreelstudio.eu`
  (unknown parts fall back to `superspl.at/scene/<id>`).
- **Notice**: the first recording of a scan outside the owner's catalogue in a page shows one line on
  the recording bar: the scan belongs to its author (name, licence), the video is the pilot's and so is
  how they use it, credit the author; "Don't show again" sets `recording.authorNotice` off (Settings ->
  Recording turns it back on). It never blocks the recording.
- D36 in `docs/decisions.md` records the change; the browser acceptances that used the gate as a
  control (`accept-d.ts`, `accept-v03-rec.ts`) now check the opposite.

## 3. Crosshair (pilot idea)

Settings (group Display, `packages/prefs/src/defs/crosshair.ts`): `crosshair.shape` off / dot / cross /
circle / ring with dot (default **off**), `crosshair.size` (% of the view's shorter side),
`crosshair.color`, `crosshair.opacity`. `apps/fly/src/crosshair.ts` (pure) turns them into SVG;
`app/builtin/crosshair.ts` puts it over the centre of the view. Hidden in cinema mode and in the clean
view. **Never in recordings**: it is a page overlay and the recorder takes only the drawn scan; a
shared clip shows the place, not a game HUD. No setting for it.

## 4. Motor sound (pilot idea)

`apps/fly/src/audio/motor-sound.ts`: Web Audio from the simulated motors, read once a frame
(`sim.s[S.m0..m3]`, the pack voltage, the motor KV; never written, so sim determinism and the input
path are untouched): per motor an oscillator with the rotor's harmonics (3-blade props: the 3rd is
the blade pass) at its own speed (the four beat against each other as in a real quad), plus looped
noise through a band-pass that follows the mean speed (prop wash). Master gain 0 when disarmed,
crashed, paused, in a menu or a hidden tab. The AudioContext is created and resumed on the first
click or key (a gamepad press is not a user activation).

Settings (new group Sound, `defs/sound.ts`): `sound.motors` on/off, `sound.volume`. **Default on at
40 %**: FPV pilots fly by ear (the motors tell throttle and load before the picture does), every FPV
simulator ships with it on, and the pilot who asked said flying is nicer with it; 40 % is a quiet
level, it is silent until the pilot clicks or presses a key, and the pause menu has "Motor sound: on /
off". Not in recordings in v0.7 (the recorder writes video only).
