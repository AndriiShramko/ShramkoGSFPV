## Root causes (with evidence)

locally, on the owner's PC (30 Hz display, system Chrome 155). Evidence `evidence/2026-10-17/v09-load-probe-base*.json`.

1. **Single-file scans wait 30 s after their last byte.** `SessionLoader.watchCoarse` revealed on
   `ready && loading === 0` only after it had seen `loading > 0`, else after 30 s of silence. A single SOG file
   (`meta.json`) is whole before the engine draws it and the engine never reports a file loading for it (the
   frame:ready loading count is the octree streamer's). Measured: d8ba72a6 every frame `ready, loading 0`
   (908 frames), revealed at 33.3 s; 224aba38 at 35.5 s; 15a202e6 after an in-page switch at 38.3 s. Meanwhile the
   card said "Preparing the view", at 10 s "taking longer than usual", at 20 s offered Try again / another scan.
   SuperSplat: 62 % of scenes are meta-only.
   - **"scan 15a202e6" and "scan 224aba38" are scenes in their own right**, single files: 147.6 MB (+ 0.7 MB walls =
     the owner's 148.3 MB) and 61.5 MB (no walls), measured with HEAD on every image. The owner was flying b11e45d1
     and a8cfc66e (both streamed, both load in 2-3 s in every condition tried) and switched to these.
   - **The lemons scene is d8ba72a6** ("The Fruit Bowl": oranges, lemons; SuperSplat search orange/lemon/citrus/
     fruit through our proxy): items 8 and "the lemons" are one case, the same cause.
   - Not reproduced as a cause: streamed scans fresh, cached (revisit), after an in-page switch from another scan,
     stochastic with `budget=8`, a 3840 x 2160 backbuffer (`VIEW=4k`): all revealed in 1.7-3.0 s on the base.
2. **The export waited for the whole finest level.** It drew `setDetail('final')` (the finest level everywhere) and
   encoded a frame only when the engine reported nothing loading. On 1994c65c (60 M splats, 11 levels) frame 0
   waited until all 1970 nodes were at level 0: drawn 0.8 -> 59.7 M splats over 70 s on this link, then the rest of
   the frames ran at the display's rate (`v09-load-probe-base-export.json`). On the owner's link and with his longer
   flight it sat 10 min at frame 0; the configured encoder stayed idle all that time and Chrome reclaimed it. A first
   run here also lost the WebGPU device under that load (the readback's mapAsync failed: "A valid external Instance
   reference no longer exists"), which ended the export the same way.
3. **The canary** checked the S3 path that answers 403 since 2026-10-16 (would open an issue every day).
4. Latent, fixed on the way: a session disposed while its first view was watched could later call
   `revealFullDetail()` on the page's shared renderer, opening the next scan's LOD range before its reveal.

## Design

- `apps/fly/src/session/reveal.ts` (pure, `RevealWatch`): the state decides each frame and every 250 ms (a hidden
  tab draws no frames): **ready** = the first view's files are all in (render-pc `firstView()`: per-file bytes),
  the engine drew splats and loads nothing; **settled** = the files are in (or it drew and loads nothing) for
  `SETTLE_MS` 2.5 s, shown anyway (the engine may go on prefetching the next level or the environment); **pilot** =
  Fly now (offered once something is drawn and the wait lasted 5 s); **stall** = 30 s with no byte and no change.
  Content type, cache and the shared renderer no longer matter. `partial` (and the explanation on the card) only for
  pilot/stall before the files were in.
- Loading card (`ui/loading.ts`): a spinner and the bar's sweep always move (reduced motion: a pulse); the seconds
  count; download: MB, MB/s, time left (unchanged); while preparing: parts in place x/y, M splats on screen, files on
  their way, "shown in N s at most" when the settle bound runs; the stall hint counts from the last change of those
  numbers; Fly now (`FlyNow` from the card to the loader); a forced reveal says why. `hook.loading` carries view,
  reveal, canFlyNow, revealInMs for the benches.
- Export (`videoexport.ts`, `builtin/video-export.ts`, `cinema.ts`): `setDetail('auto')` under cinema's budget (the
  engine's own LOD, as SuperSplat draws); a frame waits for a complete scan at most `FIRST_WAIT_MS` 20 s (frame 0)
  / `FRAME_WAIT_MS` 4 s, then is saved with what is resident (`forced`, told at the end); the bar says frame k of n
  and what it waits for (files on their way + when it goes on anyway, the encoder, a hidden tab); `EncoderRevival`
  makes a reclaimed, empty encoder again (key frame next; the same stream description required); a failed export
  offers Try again.
- Canary: CloudFront paths (settings, lod-meta, meta, voxel json/bin); bench walls from CloudFront.
