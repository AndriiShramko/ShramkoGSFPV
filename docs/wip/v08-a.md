# v0.8 lane A: play — keyboard, touch, quieter sound, easy play

The owner's items 1, 2, 3 and 6 of message 21 ([v08-plan.md](v08-plan.md)). Branch `v08-a`. Radios,
gamepads and HID devices are lane B's and keep their arm switch and full control; nothing here changes
`packages/sim-core` (the A6 hash stays `1d01ca5b...`).

## 0. What the owner said, and what was found

1. **Keyboard is "impossible to control" for gamers.** Today's keys are a Mode 2 radio laid on the
   keyboard (W/S throttle that stays where it is left, A/D yaw, arrows pitch/roll, Space arm), every
   key bang-bang (0.6 stick the moment it goes down). A gamer must first find Space, then find the
   hover throttle by ear, then fly two half-sticks at once. Research (2026-10-16): there is no standard
   keyboard layout for drone games; the radio-on-keys layout is what pilot-oriented sims use
   (Dronesim, DJI Flight Simulator), and Liftoff's developer says keyboard flying is "practically
   impossible" for a realistic sim. Games for players use **WASD to move/tilt and the arrows for turn
   and altitude** (GoDrone's web UI: arrows up/down altitude, left/right yaw, W/S pitch, A/D roll; "The
   Dude": WASD tilt, arrows turn, Space/V up/down), often with Shift as a boost (NEXUS FPV) and an
   **altitude hold** (Dronesim's Shift). Sources: webxos.itch.io/nexus-fpv, bitona.itch.io/dronesim,
   godrone.readthedocs.io (first flight), steamcommunity.com/app/410340 (Liftoff, developer's answer).
2. **Touch sticks felt different from the radio.** Measured in code (section 3): the stick-to-setpoint
   path is the same (channels go into the same rates; no deadband or expo on either side; touch
   events are handled at their own timestamps), but the pads were **absolute**: a finger landing
   off-centre is an instant step on that stick, and every new touch on the left pad **set the throttle
   to where the thumb landed**. A radio never does either: the thumb lands on the stick where it is.
3. **Motor sound too loud by default.**
6. **Phones, tablets, keyboard: players, not pilots** — no arming; fun; feel like FPV; train for real sticks.

## 1. Play profile (`input.play`: `easy` by default, or `pilot`)

Chosen by the input type: **touch and keyboard** fly the profile in `input.play`; a radio, gamepad or
HID device always flies as a radio (arm switch, its own throttle). `pilot` is the old behaviour
(radio on keys / ARM button), for pilots who want it.

| | easy (default) | pilot |
|---|---|---|
| arming | automatic: the first flying key or touch starts the motors (throttle held low until the model says armed, so the sim's arm rule is met; a refused edge is retried) | Space / ARM button, throttle low, as a radio |
| keyboard | game layout: W/S forward/back, A/D left/right, arrows left/right (or Q/E) turn, arrows up/down climb/sink, Shift faster (held with a flying key it is the boost, and shortcuts work as if it were up), Space: a tap starts the motors, a 0.5 s hold stops them (many games climb on Space: a tap in the air never drops the drone) | Mode 2 layout: W/S throttle, A/D yaw, arrows pitch/roll (unchanged) |
| keys | smooth ramps (0 -> target in 0.25 s, back in 0.12 s), targets in physical units turned into stick through the drone's own rates: tilt 25 deg (Shift 45), acro 200 deg/s (Shift 400), turn 110 deg/s (Shift 220) | bang-bang 0.6 / 0.5 (unchanged) |
| throttle | altitude help on: hold height, climb/sink rate from the keys or the left stick (spring-centred); off: stays where it is left | stays where it is left |

**Why keys, not mouse steering.** Mouse-look needs pointer lock (a click captures the cursor), which
fights the page's own buttons (the top bar, the HUD chips, the school list) and loses itself on every
Esc; a laptop's trackpad is poor at holding a steady turn; and the drone games players know use the
keys alone. WASD + arrows works on every keyboard, one hand can fly flat (WASD + Q/E) and the other
holds the height (the arrows).

**Altitude help** (`input.altitudeHelp`, on; only in easy play and in angle mode, `helpFlies`): a vertical-speed
controller in the page (`apps/fly/src/play/assist.ts`, pure): climb 2.5 m/s (Shift 5), sink 2 m/s
(Shift 4), and the height held when the command is released. Feed-forward from the model's own
numbers: thrust = mass (g + a) / cos(tilt), motor = sqrt(thrust / (4 tmax(V))), through idle and the
throttle curve (or the auto-throttle map) back to a stick; PI on the vertical speed. Tilted past 60 deg
it asks for no more than twice the level thrust; past 78 deg (on its side, upside down: `UPRIGHT_MIN_COS`)
it gives idle and holds its integral, because thrust there pushes the craft down. Not in horizon or
acro: they flip at full stick, and the review measured a horizon flip with the first help (which flew in
horizon and kept thrust on upside down) losing 3.6-5.0 m against 1.8-2.7 m with idle. **Assists are
inputs to the sim**: the stick they compute goes through Controls and the arm gate into the flight log
like any other source, so replays stay exact without the assist.

**Pilot school** (`apps/fly/src/play/school.ts`): four steps read from the settings, so there is
nothing to get out of step: 1 angle + altitude help, 2 angle (own throttle), 3 horizon (own throttle), 4 acro;
as the help flies in angle mode only, the step shown is always what flies (M from step 1 to horizon is step 3). Each
step has a goal in seconds of flight without a crash (30, 45, 60, 60); the HUD chip shows the step and
the progress; reaching it offers the next step (Next / Stay). After step 4: real sticks next (Controls).

**Haptics** (`input.haptics`, on): a short vibration on a crash with touch sticks (Android Chrome;
iOS Safari has no Vibration API).

## 2. Keyboard key card

The card shows while the craft waits on the ground (first time and every respawn that needs a
start) and on demand with **K** (keymap `help.keys`); it lists the layout of the profile in use.
K toggles what is on screen (`ui/key-card-state.ts`): a card that is up goes away (on the ground it
stays away until take-off, and the next ground wait shows it again), one that is not comes up and
stays through the flight until K again.

## 3. Touch: the measurement and the fix

What was compared (touch vs a calibrated radio, same drone and rates):

| | touch (v0.7) | radio | after |
|---|---|---|---|
| stick -> channel -> setpoint | linear, then the drone's rates | linear (`mapFrame`), then the same rates | unchanged: equal for the same deflection (unit test against `mapFrame`) |
| deadband / expo | none / none in the input | profile deadband 0 (the wizard's), expo in the rates | unchanged |
| travel for full stick | pad/2 - 24 px: **51 px** on a 375 px phone, **86 px** on a tablet or a phone on its side | ~20 mm of gimbal | unchanged, except where the screen's edge is nearer: full deflection there at the edge (8 px in), so it is reachable from any touch-down (the first grab reached 0.42 of full stick toward the edge from a phone pad's rim, 0.26 on a tablet: bench T10) |
| touch-down | **absolute**: a finger off-centre = a step (0.5 / 0.4 stick in the bench's example); a touch on the left pad **set the throttle to where the thumb landed** | the thumb lands on the stick where it is | **grab** (`play/touch-map.ts`): a touch-down never moves a stick; v0.7's jump is the control |
| when a sample reaches the flight | `pointermove` with its coalesced samples: Chrome dispatches it just before the frame whose physics step takes the samples (by their own timestamps) | a HID report or the 500 Hz gamepad poll is handled between frames and waits for the same next frame's step | unchanged (`pointermove`). Measured (bench T5): for every touch sample, the frame whose step took it, seen by a `pointermove` and a `pointerrawupdate` listener (the earliest a page can handle input, as a radio's report is): **the same frame for 100 % of samples** on all three sizes, sample to flight p50 about 50 ms with 33 ms frames (a 30 Hz display); control: a sample held to the next animation frame is measured a frame later |
| throttle | sticky | sticky | sticky in the pilot profile and easy play without the help; easy play with the help: a spring-centred climb/sink stick |

So the owner's impression was right about the sticks themselves: every new touch could jump them
(the throttle above all), and a thumb on a pad's rim could not reach full stick toward the screen's
edge. It was not the delivery: a touch reaches the flight at the same frame as a radio's report
arriving at the same moment would. (The first version of this lane read moves from
`pointerrawupdate` and said touch had been a frame late; it compared only the handler's delay, not
the frame that applies the sample, and the review of 2026-10-16 refuted it; withdrawn, and the
listener is `pointermove` again.) The numbers are the page's own handling of CDP-synthesised touches
(`accept-v08-play.ts`); a real tablet adds its digitiser's latency (typically one or two 120 Hz
scans), which only a real tablet can measure.

## 4. Sound

`sound.volume` 40 -> 15 %: master gain (v/100)^1.5 x 0.7 goes 0.177 -> 0.041, 12.8 dB quieter (about
-28 dB from full scale): a background whine that tells throttle by ear without filling the room. A
pilot who never touched the slider gets the new default (A.6); one who set it keeps it. Quick mute: a
speaker button on the top bar (phones have no keyboard) next to the pause menu's item and Settings.

  Space) and climbs at 2.63 m/s (asked 2.5); keys released, the height holds within 0.04 m over 4 s;
  W tilts 25.0 deg and the height moves 0.07 m; K shows / hides the card; 30 s without a crash offers
  step 2, whose throttle takes over from the help without a jump (0.283 -> stays). Control: the pilot
  profile does not start the motors on the same key and shows the Mode 2 card.
- Touch, easy play, tablet 1024x768, phone 375x812, phone on its side 812x375: no ARM button; a
  touch-down off-centre moves nothing (control: v0.7's pad read 0.5 / 0.4); a touch starts the motors;
  the left stick at 60 % climbs 1.64-1.66 m/s, springs back, the height holds within 0.04 m; the right
  stick at half tilts 17-18 deg (the ACTUAL rates' curve); nothing at the top prints over anything
  (control: the school chip moved onto the buttons is caught). Control: the pilot profile shows ARM
  and a touch starts nothing.
- Sound: 15 % by default; the speaker mutes / unmutes (aria-pressed).
- Node (`tools/bench/test/app-play.test.ts`, the real Sim and ArmGate, page emulated at 60 Hz frames
  and 100 Hz input): the height holds within 0.3 m for 20 s on all six presets, on the Moon, in
  auto-throttle mode and on a 25 % pack; through a 25 deg tilt within 0.6 m while the frozen hover stick
  loses more than 2 m (control); arm + hover throttle in one frame is refused by the gate and the model

runs into the voltage line (both are v0.7's).

## How to continue (for the lead and the next agent)

- Assists live in `apps/fly/src/play/` and are **inputs**: they compute sticks that go through
  `Controls.channels` and the arm gate into the log. Never put an assist into `packages/sim-core`.
- Touch and keyboard read `input.play`; a radio, gamepad or HID device never does (lane B's devices
  keep their arm switch). `?lat=1` forces the pilot profile.
- A bench that arms by hand (Space, the ARM button) must add `&set.input.play=pilot` (done for B14,
  accept-v03-modes/rec/respawn/summary, accept-v07-d1/wind-ceiling, screens.ts).
- New flying keys go into `play/keys.ts` and `FLYING_CODES` (`packages/prefs/src/keymap.ts`) and
  `FLYING` in `scripts/gen-catalog.ts`; the keymap check refuses a shortcut on a flying key.
- For docs/decisions.md (the lead numbers it): "Easy play's assists are inputs, not physics"; "The
  altitude help flies in angle mode only and gives idle when the craft is tipped past 78 deg". (No
  entry for touch delivery: pointermove and pointerrawupdate reach the flight at the same frame.)
